## Prompt set

Shared instruction: preserve the original scene's composition, perspective, objects, meaning, deep petrol palette, translucent faceted constellation structures, fine ivory/teal lines and restrained coral nodes. Replace only constellation human figures or arms with anatomically coherent solid cream/teal Communitygeeks astronauts; preserve the dark glossy visors with the approved warm ivory hair, brow and glasses projected inside. Match the approved train study's relationship of solid character to constellation world. No text, logos, generic eyeglasses, extra limbs, flat diagrams or giant mascots.

1. **Platform ecosystem:** Keep the left discussion circle, lower-left working table, rising documents, network arcs, lower-right transparent room and elevated far-right handoff. Put small astronauts in the human roles in each location. Preserve the open-versus-owned-space distinction.
2. **Cookie sharing:** Keep the open constellation jar, lids, cookies, smaller food box and one cookie in transit. Replace the two constellation arms with two astronauts who offer and receive that cookie; keep the handoff central and visible.
3. **Observatory:** Keep the angled control desk, coral decision point, coral lever, plotted route, orbital arcs and target star. Replace the constellation operator with one astronaut who touches the point and holds the lever.
4. **Telescope:** Keep the distinct constellation telescope and tripod, red adjustment knob, distant coral star and wide negative space. Replace the constellation observer with one astronaut looking through the eyepiece and adjusting the knob.
