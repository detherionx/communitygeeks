What helps builders make your technology useful?

Developers turn models and infrastructure into applications, integrations and working systems. We study the support, relationships and responsibilities around that work.

Read the four theses

Four working theses

Working theses from comparative research, practitioner conversations and public evidence. Each pairs an argument and an example with the decision it points to for a product, developer relations or partnerships team.

  • Access needs a route to a working project.

    Credits and API access create an entry point. Between a first call and a working project, builders hit different obstacles, and working examples, technical help and other builders address different ones. Nebius’s free AI Builder Program combines partner credits and discounts, office hours, cookbooks and community events. What it offers is documented; which part helps is not.

    Find where builders get stuck between first access and a useful implementation, and support that step.

    Nebius AI Builder Program (Nebius, September 2026)

  • Contributions need a route into use.

    Integrations, guides and fixes become useful when people can find, review and use them. Fireworks lists partner integrations in its documentation after its developer relations team reviews them. Zed’s 12-week Guild paired 33 active contributors with its engineers; Zed reports 148 merged pull requests from the first cohort. Both make the route into the product explicit.

    Decide who reviews, responds to and maintains contributions before you invite more of them.

    Fireworks developer partner process · Zed Guild retrospective (Zed, June 2026)

  • Support should fit what builders want to do.

    A startup shipping an application, a maintainer fixing an integration and an organiser teaching peers need different things: credits and technical help, review and recognition, event funding and materials. Anthropic’s Claude Community Ambassadors support local organisers with event funding, content and API credits. One ambassador programme or Discord server does not cover every relationship.

    Choose the participants and the work you will support before choosing a programme format.

    Claude Community Ambassadors (Anthropic) · Nebius Fellows (Nebius)

  • Useful work creates ongoing responsibilities.

    An integration may need maintenance, an answer may need documentation and a contributor may need to step back. Ultimate PDF, a community-built OutSystems component, shows other components and projects depending on work its creator and community keep running, with support described as best-effort. The case sets out who builds, reuses and supports it, and what is still unknown.

    Agree support boundaries, ownership and continuity, and check whether useful work stays usable over time.

    Ultimate PDF case study

Builder support does not replace community management. It extends the work across community, developer relations, partnerships and product, and it holds up when each team knows which part it owns.
