Research›AI & AI infrastructure›Ultimate PDF

One feature. Many hands.

Ultimate PDF turns web pages into reports and invoices. Developers build with it. Other components reuse it. Its creator and community help keep it working.

The capability belongs to the product experience. The work extends beyond the company.

Follow the evidence

  1. Built and reused. The Forge listing names Leonardo Fernandes and a wider team, lists Simple Reports PDF as an asset consumer, and includes a developer’s account of using it for reports and invoices. Component, team & review
  2. Maintained. After a developer reported errors following a platform upgrade, a peer offered advice and Leonardo requested diagnostics. The thread does not confirm a fix. The support exchange
  3. A support boundary. Forge explicitly describes community support as best-effort; the component is not supported by OutSystems.

Public evidence reviewed 25 October 2026. Maintenance hours, compensation and succession arrangements are unknown. This case does not imply that anyone plans to leave.

If a maintainer steps back, who looks after the people building on their work?
