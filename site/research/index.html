Research

Why players stay. Why builders build.

We study how players, developers and partners make products more useful, and what companies need to support that work.

Players add to what a studio makes.

Players create guides, organise groups, test builds and sometimes build new experiences.

The question is which of that work helps people discover, understand or enjoy the game, and what the studio should support.

Gaming research

Access is the beginning of building.

Developers need to make a technology work in their own projects. Examples, integrations, technical help and other builders can support that progress.

The question is which support produces useful work that others can build on.

AI & AI infrastructure research

What this means for community

Community work no longer ends at the edge of a company-owned channel. People contribute through their own projects, groups, tutorials, integrations and relationships, often in places the company does not run.

They have goals of their own. They are not a company’s unpaid distribution network, and support works when it helps them do what they came to do.

That spreads responsibility. Community, developer relations, partnerships and product teams each need to know who responds to this work and who supports it. Judge it by what participants can accomplish and what becomes useful to others, alongside the business outcomes that matter to you.

Start with what people are trying to do. Then decide what your company should support.

How the research works

We compare how companies design and run programmes for the people around their products, from public documentation and company-reported results. We talk with practitioners in community, developer relations, partnerships and product roles, and we run our own experiments.

We keep documented programme design, what companies report and our own interpretation apart, and we treat open questions as open. Our first completed experiment tested how what people publish about a product shows up in answers from ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity.
