About

Why players care. Why builders stay.

Communitygeeks works in two markets: game studios that need players to care, and AI companies that need developers to build on their technology. We research what makes that happen, then help companies put it into practice.

Builders write the integrations, tools and applications that make a model or platform useful. Many also teach, run meetups and answer each other’s questions.

What connects them

In both markets, people outside the company add value it did not build: they build, teach, organise and recommend, often where the company is not looking.

We help companies decide which of that work to support, and run it with them at a size their team can keep up.
