Find your first players, then grow them

A practical session for Loading Zone studios.

Lisboa Games Week · 19 and 20 November 2026 · Carmelito Bauer

Two working sessions with student studios from Universidade Lusófona at the Loading Zone. Each team pitched its game, and we worked through who it’s for, who has played it outside class, and what to do next. The two questions that came up most: how to post without sounding like a brand, and how to get ready for Steam Next Fest.
