Scope, access, responsibilities and exclusions

What the engagement includes, what you provide and what is not included.

  • Player Panel: one game; in the first two weeks we pick 50 players with your team, from people in your Discord, playtests and wishlists who already take part and from players of similar games; one playtest round a month on your current build; a decision report within a week of each round (what broke, where players quit, what to change next); a public changelog of what changed because of the panel, with credit by name.
  • Access: a build for each round and one contact on your team who joins the picking and receives the reports; approvals within five working days.
  • Fees and term: from €4,500 a month per game, as agreed in writing before the first month, invoiced monthly. Three-month minimum. After the minimum, either side can end the panel with 30 days’ notice.
  • Player rewards and keys: rewards, keys, merchandise and any payments to players are paid by you, directly, and are not part of our fee.
  • Reviews: players never receive anything in exchange for store reviews or ratings. Credit and early access are for playtesting and feedback only, in line with Steam’s rules on incentivised reviews.
  • Delays: if client-side delays push a phase past its planned end, we re-plan on dates agreed with our availability, and agree any remobilisation work before resuming.
  • Not included: ongoing moderation or community management, paid media and influencer campaigns, trailers, store pages, game development, event production, travel, third-party costs and sales outcomes.
