Scope, access, responsibilities and exclusions

What each engagement includes, what you provide and what is not included.

  • Programme Check: one question about your players or community, agreed with you in the kickoff call, and one owner on your side; 3–4 interviews in total with people from your team or your community; interviewee introductions and what you already run (programme pages, community channels, relevant numbers) by day 3; two weeks from kickoff to walkthrough. You get a short written map and a 60-minute walkthrough with your team. Implementation is not included. €4,000 at kickoff. Start a Decision Review within 60 days of the walkthrough and the €4,000 is deducted from its fee.
  • Decision Review: one decision, its owner and the person who approves the spend; 8–10 interviews in total; interviewee introductions by day 3, data and tool access by day 5; the approver joins the decision session. €14,000 at kickoff.
  • Programme Build: one programme for one primary group in your community; a named sponsor and a community lead with about four hours a week from week 5; approvals within five working days. €14,000 at kickoff; after the week-4 stop gate, €28,000 invoiced as €14,000 at week 5 and €14,000 at week 10.
  • Rewards, keys and creator fees: creator fees, keys, rewards, merchandise and any payments to players are paid by you, directly, and are not part of our fee.
  • Delays: if client-side delays push a phase past its planned end, we re-plan on dates agreed with our availability, and agree any remobilisation work before resuming.
  • Progress review: one session ninety days after handover, based on what your team reports, with a short note. It does not prove lasting impact.
  • Programme Support: scope, cadence, fees and term agreed in writing before work starts.
  • Not included: ongoing moderation or community management, paid media and influencer campaigns, trailers, store pages, game development, event production, travel, third-party costs and sales outcomes.
