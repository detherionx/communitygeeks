Scope, access, responsibilities and exclusions

What each engagement includes, what you provide and what is not included.

  • Programme Check: one question about the people building on your product, agreed with you in the kickoff call, and one owner on your side; 3–4 interviews in total with people from your team, users or partners; interviewee introductions and what you already run (programme pages, docs, relevant numbers) by day 3; two weeks from kickoff to walkthrough. You get a short written map (what is happening, where help would matter, what to do next) and a 60-minute walkthrough with your team. If the question needs more interviews, or a decision with a spend approver, we recommend a Decision Review instead. Implementation is not included. €4,000 at kickoff. Start a Decision Review within 60 days of the walkthrough and the €4,000 is deducted from its fee.
  • Decision Review: one decision, its owner and the person who approves the spend; 8–10 interviews in total; interviewee introductions by day 3, data and tool access by day 5; the approver joins the decision session. €14,000 at kickoff.
  • Programme Build: one intervention for one primary audience; a named sponsor and an operator with about four hours a week from week 5; approvals within five working days. €14,000 at kickoff; after the week-4 stop gate, €28,000 invoiced as €14,000 at week 5 and €14,000 at week 10.
  • Builder cohort: one contribution track and a small group of builders. We select the builders with you, speak with each of them, shape the cohort with them and your team, support the first cycle and hand it over. Your operator runs the cohort day to day, and an engineer on your team reviews contributions and answers technical questions while it runs. Credits or payments to participants are your cost.
  • Delays: if client-side delays push a phase past its planned end, we re-plan on dates agreed with our availability, and agree any remobilisation work before resuming.
  • Progress review: one session ninety days after handover, based on what your team reports, with a short note. It does not prove lasting impact.
  • Programme Support: scope, cadence, fees and term agreed in writing before work starts.
  • Not included: ongoing operation or moderation, software builds beyond configuring existing tools, event production, paid media, travel, third-party costs and hiring outcomes.
