Grow the builder ecosystem around your product.

We plan, launch and improve programmes with your team for the people who build products, workflows and businesses with your technology.

See the four ways to workBook a conversation

What we help you build.

Already running a programme? We review what works and plan the next stage, from relaunching ambassadors to keeping support answers findable.

  • New users who never get started

    Help new developers ship their first project

    Starter projects, office hours and a place to show what they built, so sign-ups become active developers.

  • Developers already sending feedback

    Start a small cohort of your most active builders

    A handful of builders test releases, report problems and build with your team. We run the first cycle with you, then hand it over.

  • Users already building add-ons

    Launch a programme for integrations and mods

    Clear rules for submitting work, a review process, and a place where other users can find what gets built.

  • Users who already teach others

    Give your best contributors a bigger role

    An ambassador or expert programme with clear roles, real recognition and support from your team, or a fix for the one you run.

From first conversation to a programme your team runs.

  1. 1

    Talk to your team and your users

    We interview your team and the people around your product to learn what helps them get started and keep going.

    A clear picture of what people need.

  2. 2

    Recommend the right programme

    We compare options on cost, likely effect and your team’s time, then recommend one. It may be a change to what you already run.

    Costed options and one recommendation with a named owner.

  3. 3

    Launch it and hand it over

    We design the programme with the person who will run it and launch it together. We see it through its first weeks, adjust it once and hand it over.

    A working programme your team can run.

One question first? Start with a two-week Programme Check.

Programme Check

€4,000fixed fee

2 weeks3–4 interviews

For teams with one concrete question about the people building on their product, who want an outside view before committing to a programme.

What’s included
  • We agree the one question with you in a kickoff call.
  • We interview 3–4 people from your team, users or partners, and review what you already run.
  • You get a short written map: what is happening, where help would matter, and what to do next, including stopping or doing it yourselves.
  • A 60-minute walkthrough with your team.

Continue to a Decision Review within 60 days and the €4,000 counts toward it.

Book a Programme Check

Decision Review

€14,000fixed fee

4 weeks8–10 interviews in total

For teams deciding which programme to launch, change or stop, and who should run it.

● Go ahead■ Change course▲ Hold off

What’s included
  • We interview 8–10 people from your team, users and partners, and review your data.
  • We show you what they need and what your team can realistically support.
  • You get costed options and one recommendation with a named owner, agreed with whoever approves the budget.

The Review is the first phase of the Programme Build. Continue with an agreed scope for a further €28,000.

Programme Build

€42,000fixed fee, one stop gate

13 weeksIncludes the 4-week Review

For one programme you want running by a set date, designed and launched with your team.

What’s included
  • €14,000 to start · weeks 1–4: the Decision Review.
  • €28,000 to continue · weeks 5–13: two €14,000 instalments, at weeks 5 and 10. Design, launch, the first weeks of use, one adjustment and handover.

Stop gate after week 4Stop here and keep the decision, or approve the build.

Includes one progress review 90 days after handover, based on your team’s reports.
