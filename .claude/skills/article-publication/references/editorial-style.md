# Editorial style

- Preserve the article's meaning, uncertainty, names, companies, source links, and evidence level.
- Communitygeeks authored copy does not use em dashes. Rewrite with a period, comma, colon, or semicolon that matches the sentence. Do not mechanically replace every em dash with a hyphen. Preserve exact quotations; if a quotation contains an em dash, flag it instead of silently altering it.
- Do not display `OBS. 001`, `OBS. 002`, or similar identifiers. Internal ordering may remain in data when required by the journal animation.
- `deck` is the canonical visible subtitle on the homepage, Public Thinking overview, and article page. Make it self-contained, descriptive, and consistent across all three surfaces. State the subject and finding in plain language. Name a person or company only when it materially helps discovery and remains accurate.
- `summary` is the meta description and Article schema description. Aim for 140 to 170 characters. It must accurately summarize the page and invite a click without hype.
- Use clear H2 sections for distinct claims. Make people, companies, products, and key concepts explicit in prose so search engines and answer engines can resolve them.
- Cite primary sources where the article relies on public claims. Do not add citations that are not actually evidence for the sentence.
